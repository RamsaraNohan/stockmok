The problem
Stock lives in a spreadsheet, orders live in messages, and the two are reconciled by memory. Nobody can say what changed a balance, or when, or who did it.
What Stockmok does
Every balance is the signed sum of its movements. Every movement names an actor, a reason and a time. Purchase orders and receipts write to that same ledger — nothing else can.
What is in the product
Inventory control
Products, categories, warehouses, per-location balances, minimums and an immutable movement history.
Procurement
Purchase orders against private suppliers, partial receipts, over-receipt blocked before submission.
Connected businesses
Connect by handle, map a supplier's item to yours with a stated conversion, order in their unit.
Analytics
Inventory value, stock status, movement and purchase-order reports that reconcile to the ledger.
