The five states of an order you place yourself
Draft → Ordered → Partially received → Received Cancelled
Draft — being written. Nothing is committed and stock is untouched. Editable in full.
Ordered — you have placed it with the supplier. Lines are fixed; the order is now waiting for goods.
Partially received — some of it has arrived and is in your stock. The rest is still outstanding.
Received — every line is complete. The order becomes history and can no longer be changed.
Cancelled — abandoned from Draft or from Ordered, only while nothing has been received. Once any quantity has arrived, cancelling is refused and the reason is shown; the order is finished by receiving the rest.
This list is the whole record
Four orders exist in this workspace. Every figure elsewhere in Gate 7 comes from these four rows, and the two received orders are the two that appear in the movement ledger.
A partly delivered order, in its own moment
On 13 August nothing is outstanding, so no amber row appears here. The Partially received state is drawn where it actually happened, on 7f and 7g, dated 4 August.
Connected orders sit in the same list
CPO-2026-003 is one you placed through the network. It shares this list and this page header, but has its own longer set of states, drawn in Gate 9.

State is a word, a colour and a consequence. Every pill is accompanied by a plain sentence saying what can still be done, and the one conditional transition — cancelling — is stated as a rule about received quantity rather than as a permission.

The rules this form is enforcing
Seven figures, always all seven
Ordered, already received, receive now, the store room, the balance now, the balance after, and what remains outstanding. Not one of them is left to be worked out in someone's head, and the resulting order state is spelt out as a word.
Receiving is the only thing that moves stock
Placing an order changed nothing. This screen is where 40.000 KG becomes real, and it writes exactly one line in the movement history, visible immediately on the product.
One store room per receipt
If a delivery is split between two rooms, it is two receipts against the same order, each with its own line and its own resulting balance. Nothing is silently divided.
A receipt cannot be edited away
If 40.000 was recorded and 38.000 actually arrived, the answer is a correction with a reason, which leaves both entries in the history. There is no quiet rewrite.
Plain words only
Nothing on this screen names a mechanism. There is no reference to how repeated taps are handled, no operation identifier, and no internal movement type — the person receiving goods is told what happens to the stock, and that is all.

Matches the ledger exactly. 20.000 + 40.000 = 60.000 KG in Cold Room, the balance recorded against 4 Aug 13:48 in the Gate 6 movement history, with 10.000 KG left outstanding and the order state named before the button is pressed.

An order may pass straight from Ordered to Received when the whole quantity arrives at once. Partially received is a state, not a step everyone must take.
Nothing returns from Received. A completed order is history; a mistake inside it is answered on the product, with a correction that carries its reason.
There is no state for money. Ordered does not mean invoiced, and Received does not mean paid — this product tracks goods.
