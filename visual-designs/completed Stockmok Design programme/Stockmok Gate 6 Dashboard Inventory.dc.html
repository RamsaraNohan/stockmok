Row menu · what is offered
Open · Edit · Adjust stock · View movements · Archive. There is no delete: a product with history is archived so its movements stay readable.

Plain words for every kind
Opening balance · Received · Issued · Transferred in · Transferred out · Correction. The Kind filter offers exactly these words and nothing from the system underneath.
A transfer is two lines
Out of one store room, into another, at the same moment, each with its own resulting balance. Total stock is unchanged and the pair is visibly a pair.
Read-only, by design
There is no edit and no archive on a movement — not a disabled one. A wrong entry is answered with a correction that carries a reason.
Filtered to nothing

Every line answers three questions: what changed, in which store room, and what the balance became. Order references appear because a person needs them; nothing from the system's internals does.

The three states, defined once
Out of stock is nothing on hand anywhere. Low stock is at or below the minimum. In stock is above it. A product with no minimum set is never low — it is in stock or out.
Read across the whole workspace
Status is judged on total stock, not on one store room: 0.000 KG in the Bar Store while the Cold Room is full is not out of stock. Filtering by warehouse narrows which rows appear, never what the status means.

Export lives here and on Purchase orders
Two report surfaces only — Stock on hand and Purchase orders. Export is not a page-header habit, and it writes the rows already on screen under the current filters, with the workspace name, the filters and the timestamp in the file.
Purchase orders report

What these reports are not
No stock turnover, no valuation history, no forecast, no supplier scorecard and no cross-business comparison. Inventory value is replacement cost at today's unit costs and says so on the page, so nobody reads it as an accounting valuation.
