Tables become cards, not scrollers
The seven-column desktop list keeps only what a person in the store room acts on: name, state, quantity, where it sits, and one action. Nothing is placed behind a sideways scroll.
44 px is the floor, 48 px is the habit
Rows are 72 px, tab items 56 px, form controls and buttons 48 px, and the smallest tappable thing on any of these three screens is the 44 px new-product button. Text never drops below 13 px.
The arithmetic still shows
The adjustment sheet states now, change and after for the chosen store room, plus the resulting total across both — the same guard rail as the desktop form, in a shorter sentence.
Four destinations, not sixteen
The phone carries Home, Products, Receive and More; the remaining destinations live under More in the same order as the sidebar, so the two shapes stay learnable as one product.

Drawn at true 390 × 844. A reduction of the same 120.000 KG record — no phone-only figure, no phone-only feature, and no capability removed that the role still permits. Reason stays required on the small screen, because that is where a hurried adjustment is most likely.
