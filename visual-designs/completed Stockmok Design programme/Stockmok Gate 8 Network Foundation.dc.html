Exact handle, or nothing
A business is found by typing its handle exactly and pressing Look up. There is no browsing, no suggestions as you type, no list of nearby businesses, no category directory and no marketplace. If you do not already know who you are looking for, this product cannot help you find them — and says so.
A catalog is a projection, never a window
A Partner Catalog holds only what a business chose to publish: a name, a partner SKU, a unit and an optional price. It is not their inventory. Stock, cost, store rooms, team members, orders with others and history never leave a workspace, in any state.

The moment a connection begins
Both sides are told
A notification appears for each workspace, naming the other business and the date. Nothing else changes on either side.
Catalogs become visible

Past private orders with the same business stay private orders. Connecting does not convert history or reveal anything that happened before.

Symmetry with no leverage. Both sides see the same three facts about each other while a request waits — name, handle, message — and the accept dialog states exactly what accepting grants before it is granted, including the reassurance that an unpublished catalog shows nothing.

Publishing copies a description, never a quantity
A published item carries a name, a code your partner will use, a unit of sale and an optional price. It never carries how much you hold, what it cost you, which store room it is in, or whether you are low. Your partner sees a list of what you sell — not a view of your shelves.

Everything crossing this line is something a person typed or a state a person set. Nothing is derived, inferred, aggregated or shared for anyone's benefit but the two businesses on the order.
Receiving is one-sided
They learn that 8 PACK was received. They never learn it became 40.000 KG in your Cold Room, or that your balance went from 70 to 110.
No aggregate is a loophole
There is no "most ordered", no partner ranking, no benchmark and no total across businesses. A number computed from private data is still private data.
Disabling does not reveal
Ending a connection tells the other side only that it ended and who ended it. No reason is collected, so no reason can be disclosed.
The boundary is the same on a phone
No small-screen summary shows a figure the desktop withholds. Layout changes; the boundary does not.

Nine things cross, and they are listed exhaustively. Stating the boundary as a closed list rather than a policy sentence is what makes it testable — any screen in Gate 9 showing a tenth thing is a defect.

The supplier is not asked

What happens without one

Built in Gate 9
The five-stage flow that creates a verified mapping, its eight refusals, the connected order with its longer set of states, and receiving in the partner's unit.

The foundation, stated plainly. Two mappings, one active and one kept alive only so history still reads correctly — and the rule that a partner never sees, approves or even learns of the translation you made of their goods.
