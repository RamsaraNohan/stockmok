Stock lives in a spreadsheet. Orders live in messages.

The two are reconciled by memory, and nobody can say what changed a balance, when, or who did it. Stockmok makes the ledger the only way a balance can move.

Products, categories, warehouses, per-location balances, minimums and an immutable movement history.

Procurement

03
Connected businesses

Connect by handle, map a supplier's item to yours with a stated conversion, order in their unit.

04
Analytics

Inventory value, stock status, movement and purchase-order reports that reconcile to the ledger.

How connected supplier mapping works
STEP 1
Connect by handle

Exact handle only — @freshfoods. No marketplace, no discovery.

STEP 2
They publish an item

Five partner fields. Never their stock, costs or locations.

STEP 3
You map it to your product

You confirm it is the same real item, then state the conversion factor.

STEP 4
Order in their unit

10 PACK arrives as 50 KG in your ledger, with both shown throughout.
